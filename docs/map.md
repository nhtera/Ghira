# Map

The **Map** tab turns a meeting's notes into a mind map: the title, then one branch for each section, then one leaf for each point. Nothing new is written. It is built from the notes you already have, so it is instant and works offline.

## Read it

Open a meeting and choose **Map** next to **Notes** and **Transcript**.

- The branches are **Summary**, the template's own sections, **Decisions**, **Proposed**, **Action items**, **Open questions**, **Topics** and **Moments you marked**. Anything Ghira does not know how to place goes under **Other**.
- A leaf shows at most 80 characters. Hover it for the full text.
- Action leaves show who owns them, with their color and initial. Proposed leaves carry a **Proposed** label. A star marks a point that covers a moment you marked. See [Marks](marks.md).

## Use it

- **Click a leaf** to play its first source. **Cmd-click** (Ctrl-click on Windows) shows the line in the transcript.
- **Click a section** to collapse or expand it.
- **Drag** to move around, **scroll or pinch** to zoom, **Fit** to see everything. With the map focused, **+**, **−** and **0** zoom in, out and fit.
- **Copy as outline** puts the notes on the clipboard as a nested Markdown list.

## With the keyboard

Press Tab to reach the map, then an arrow key. A list of the same items appears over the map. **Up** and **Down** move through it, **Right** opens a section or steps into it, **Left** closes it or steps out, **Enter** plays a leaf, and **Cmd/Ctrl+Enter** shows it in the transcript. The map follows your focus. A screen reader reads the list as a tree.

Animations are off when your system asks for reduced motion.

## On iPhone

The iPhone has an **Outline** at the end of the Notes tab instead of a map: the same sections and points as a list. Tap a point to play its first source.

## Limits

The map can't be saved as an image. Use **Copy as outline**, or [Export](export.md).
