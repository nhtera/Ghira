# Speakers and voices

Ghira tells speakers apart while you record and keeps the labels in the transcript and the notes. You can name them live or later.

## How speakers are labeled

- Speakers appear in the order they talk, as **Speaker 1**, **Speaker 2** and so on. Each has a color and an initial, so you never rely on color alone.
- In a **Call**, your own microphone is **Me**. Everyone on the other side is separated from the meeting audio. Only your microphone can be Me in a call.
- In a **Room**, one microphone hears everyone, and Ghira separates all the voices, including yours.
- Live, Ghira shows up to 8 voices. More than that share an **Others** lane until the recording ends, and Ghira tells them apart after the meeting.

## Name a speaker

You can rename during the recording or in a saved meeting.

1. Select the speaker. Ghira asks **Who is this?**
2. Type a name, or pick someone already in **People**.
3. The name applies to every line of that speaker, and to the notes.

**Play 3 s sample** plays a short clip of the voice, so you can check who it is. Leave the name empty to go back to "Speaker N".

Names you give feed the custom vocabulary, so the final transcript spells them correctly. See **Learned from names you gave speakers** in **Settings → Languages**.

## Fix a mistake

Open **Speaker options** for a speaker to get the speaker panel. It offers these actions:

- **Merge** a speaker into another when one person was split in two. This cannot be undone.
- **Split speaker…** moves the lines you pick to a new person, when two people were joined.
- **Not a person (video, music)** hides the lines of a video, music or background audio from the notes. Choose **This is a person** to undo it.
- On a single line, **Edit text** fixes the words and **Change speaker** moves just that line. The notes keep their citations.

In a Room recording, **This is me** marks a speaker as you, and **Not me** undoes it.

## People

The **People** screen lists everyone you have named, how many meetings you shared, and open action items. From there you can:

- name voices you have not named yet, under **Unknown voices**
- merge two entries for one person, such as "Minh" and "Minh Nguyen" (**Merge with…**)
- remove a name from the notes (**Remove name from notes…**), which turns the lines back into "Speaker N"

## Your voice profile

A voice profile lets Ghira recognize a voice without asking. It is a numeric summary of how someone sounds. It cannot be played back as audio, it is stored encrypted on your Mac, and it is never uploaded. See [Privacy](../PRIVACY.md).

You can save a profile for yourself:

1. Download the voice model in **Settings → Models**.
2. Open **Settings → Privacy** and find **Your voice**.
3. Choose **Record your voice again…** and read the passage aloud. It takes about 20 seconds. A new recording replaces the old profile.

With a profile, Ghira can suggest and apply **Me** in Room recordings. Choose **Delete my voice data…** at any time; your name stays in the notes.

## Other people's voices

Saving other people's voice profiles is switched off in this version. You can still name anyone, and the name is kept in your meetings and in **People**. Ghira does not save their voices, and **Settings → Privacy** says so next to **Learn voices from confirmed speakers**.

Ghira also never learns a voice from a sensitive meeting. See [Recording](recording.md).

## Limits

- Voice profiles do not sync to your iPhone. They stay on each device. See [iPhone and sync](iphone-sync.md).
- Speaker separation can be wrong with overlapping speech or phone-quality audio. Use the actions above to correct it.
