# Proposed and decided

Notes should say how sure they are. Ghira sorts the decisions it finds into two kinds.

- **Decided:** someone agreed or confirmed it in the meeting ("agreed, we ship Friday").
- **Proposed:** someone suggested it and nobody settled it ("maybe we could try a dark theme").

## In the notes

The **Decisions** section lists decided items first. Proposed items follow, each with a **Proposed** chip, and the line "AI suggestions are not commitments." Answers from **Ask** carry the same line.

When Ghira is unsure it chooses **Proposed**. It is the safer way to be wrong: nothing is presented as agreed that was not.

## Change your mind

You know what was agreed. On a decision, open **Decision options** and choose **Mark as proposed** or **Mark as decided**. The item moves, and counts as **Edited by you**, so **Regenerate notes** keeps it. This works on what Ghira wrote, not on lines you typed yourself.

## Exports and search

**Export** puts proposed items in their own **Proposed** section, and the follow-up email lists them as "Proposed, not agreed". Search finds them like any other note. On iPhone they appear under Decisions after the decided ones, with the same chip.
