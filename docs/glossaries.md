# Glossaries

A glossary pack is a ready-made list of terms for a field. It helps Ghira spell what was said in the notes: a drug, a statute, a ratio, an API.

## Turn one on

Open **Settings → Languages**, find **Glossary packs**, and switch on the ones you want. There are four fields, each in English and Vietnamese:

| Field | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Medical** | Conditions, medicines, tests and procedures |
| **Legal** | Contracts, courts, property and company law |
| **Finance** | Accounting, tax, banking and investing |
| **Tech and software** | Software, cloud, security and AI |

Each pack shows how many terms it holds. Packs are bundled with Ghira, so nothing is downloaded.

## What they do, and what they don't

- The terms that were actually said in the meeting (up to 40, together with your own vocabulary and the people in the calendar event) go to the model that writes the notes, so it writes them the way they are spelled. Terms nobody said are not sent to the model.
- They **never change the transcript.** The words in the transcript stay what the speech model heard. Fix a wrong word there by hand.
- Your own **Custom vocabulary** (and the names Ghira learns from speakers) also go to the notes this way. Unlike packs, they are also used to correct the final transcript towards those words. See [Settings](settings.md).

## Sync

Which packs are on is a setting that follows you: it syncs between your paired computer and iPhone. See [iPhone and sync](iphone-sync.md).

## Privacy

Pack terms and the spellings they give stay on your device. They are not sent to a cloud model. See [Cloud AI](cloud-ai.md).
