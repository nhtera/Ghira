# Ghira 1.0 (draft)

> Draft skeleton. Lines marked **TBD** wait for an owner decision or a final
> build. Remove this box and every TBD before publishing.

Ghira is a meeting note taker that works offline, for English and Vietnamese.
It runs on your own computer or phone. Version 1.0 is the first stable release.

## Highlights

- **Record a call or a room.** On a Mac, Ghira hears your microphone and the
  meeting app's audio. On an iPhone, it records a room and keeps going with the
  screen locked. The transcript appears as people talk, in English, Vietnamese
  or both, with each speaker told apart. You can name speakers as they talk.
- **Notes written on your own device.** Summary, decisions, action items with
  owners and open questions. Every sentence links to the moment it was said, and
  one tap plays it. What you typed during the meeting is kept.
- **Find anything.** Search all meetings, with or without Vietnamese accents
  (typing `chot` finds `chốt`). Ask a question about one meeting, or across all
  of them. Organise with folders and tags, and see everything one person said.
- **Bring recordings in.** Import audio or video from Voice Memos, Zoom, Plaud
  or any file. On iPhone, share a recording straight into Ghira.
- **Share your work.** Export to Markdown, Word, text, subtitles or Obsidian,
  and draft a follow-up email.
- **Your voice, if you want it.** Ghira can learn your voice so that "Me" is
  labelled correctly. This needs your explicit consent, and you can delete it
  at any time.
- **Optional cloud help, one meeting at a time.** Use your own provider key to
  improve notes or ask questions. You see exactly what will be sent before it
  goes, names can be hidden, and audio never leaves your device.
- **English and Vietnamese interface**, light and dark.

## Privacy

- Everything stays on your device. Meetings are encrypted on disk, and deleting
  a meeting destroys its key, so the audio, transcript and notes cannot be
  recovered.
- Ghira goes online only to download its models (about 4 GB, once) **TBD: final
  size**, to check for updates (you can turn this off), and for cloud AI when
  you ask. **Strict offline** mode blocks all of it.
- No telemetry. Crash reports are written on your device and are never sent.
- Cloud AI sends transcript text only, never audio, and only after you have
  previewed it.
- Voice profiles of other people are **not** available in this version **TBD:
  confirm after legal review**.
- You can lock the app (Touch ID, Face ID or your password), choose how long
  audio is kept, export everything, or delete everything.

- Vietnamese recorded in a room (not a call) is less accurate than calls. A

- Syncing between a phone and a computer is **TBD: not in 1.0, or included
  (owner decision)**. Until then each device keeps its own meetings.
- On iPhone the notes are written with a cloud provider you choose, or on a
  computer later. Notes are not written on the phone itself.
- Older iPhones may record only, and process later while charging. Ghira tells
  you which. **TBD: list of supported models.**
- Pressing the record shortcut from the Lock Screen or Control Center is not
  available yet.
- Detecting who is speaking can be wrong with many people talking at once. You
  can rename, merge and split speakers afterwards. **TBD: merging and splitting
  speakers in a saved meeting, depending on the final build.**
