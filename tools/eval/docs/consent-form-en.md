**Collected by:** [Customer company name], [address], [contact person and email] ("we").

## What this is for

We are testing meeting-note software (speech recognition and speaker separation) on our own meetings. We record some meetings and give the recordings to the test tool, so we can measure how many mistakes it makes.

We use the recordings **only for this test**. We do not use them to train software, to assess people, or for any other purpose.

## What we record

- The audio of the meeting, including your voice.
- A written copy of what was said (made by us, with the help of software), and labels that say who spoke when.
- Your voice is **biometric, sensitive personal data**. The test may compute temporary "voice features" to check whether the software can tell speakers apart and recognise the same person in two meetings. These features are deleted with the recordings.
- In the test files your voice is labelled with a code (for example P07), not your name. Names can still appear: in the written transcripts (if someone says your name), in reference notes (for example as the owner of a task), and in a list of names that the privacy check uses to make sure no name reaches a report. The list that links codes to names is kept separately by [Data contact name]. All of this is kept on the same encrypted storage and is deleted together with the recording.

## Where it is kept, and who sees it

- The recordings, labels and transcripts are stored **encrypted, only on [Customer company name]'s own computers**.
- They are **never published** and **never sent to the Ghira team** or to anyone outside [Customer company name].
- Only the people we name can open them: [names or roles, for example the labeller and the data contact].
- What leaves [Customer company name] is a report of **aggregate numbers** (for example "the error rate was 14%"). It contains no audio, no text from your meetings and no names.

## How long we keep it, and deletion

We keep the recordings and everything made from them until **[date or number of months, for example 6 months after the test ends]**. Then we delete them, including backups, and confirm the deletion in writing to [Data contact name].

## Your rights

- You may **withdraw this consent and ask us to delete your recordings at any time**, without giving a reason.
- To do so, tell [Data contact name] at [email / phone]. We will delete your voice from the recordings, or delete the whole recording if your voice cannot be separated, within **[7] days** and confirm to you in writing.
- Withdrawing does not affect anything we did before you withdrew.
- You may ask what we hold about you and ask for a copy of it.

## It is voluntary

You do not have to agree. If you refuse, or withdraw later, **nothing bad happens to you**: it has no effect on your job, your role or your relationship with [Customer company name]. If you do not agree, we will not record you, or we will pause the recording while you speak.
